# Scout X Spotflight Design

## Style Prompt
Dark, fluid, data-driven sports-tech visual language for Scout X. Motion should feel smooth and confident, with soft momentum curves, layered depth, and clean information hierarchy. The mood is high-focus, modern, and premium without neon overload.

## Colors
- `#05090D` Night Black (canvas/background)
- `#72F52A` Radar Green (primary accent, key stats, CTA)
- `#A6FF6A` Glow Green (secondary accent, glows/highlights)
- `#FFFFFF` Pure White (primary text)
- `#D9DEE3` Soft Gray (secondary text and dividers)

## Typography
- `Inter, "SF Pro Display", "Segoe UI", sans-serif`

## What NOT to Do
- No blue/purple accents outside the brand palette.
- No hard jump cuts between scenes; always transition.
- No dense paragraph text blocks; keep copy short and punchy.
- No aggressive flashing or chaotic jitter motion.
- No low-contrast gray-on-dark body text.
